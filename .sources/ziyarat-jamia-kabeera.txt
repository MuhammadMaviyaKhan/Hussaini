220 | the firmness of your stance, | وَثَبَاتَ مَقَامِكُمْ
221 | the honor of your station and position with Him, | وَشَرَفَ مَحَلِّكُمْ وَمَنْزِلَتِكُمْ عِنْدَهُ

243 | I profess you, | مُعْتَرِفٌ بِكُمْ

295 | following your guidance, | وَيَهْتَدِي بِهُدَاكُمْ

297 | given the chance to appear again in your Return, | وَيَكِرُّ فِي رَجْعَتِكُمْ
298 | given authority in your administration, | وَيُمَلَّكُ فِي دَوْلَتِكُمْ
299 | honored to live under your sound supervision, | وَيُشَرَّفُ فِي عَافِيَتِكُمْ

309 | and the utmost of the description of your actual value, | وَمِنَ ٱلْوَصْفِ قَدْرَكُمْ
310 | since you are the light of the upright ones, | وَأَنْتُمْ نُورُ ٱلْأَخْيَارِ
311 | the guides of the pious ones, | وَهُدَاةُ ٱلْأَبْرَارِ
312 | and the arguments of the Supreme Lord. | وَحُجَجُ ٱلْجَبَّارِ

326 | all things are humiliated before you, | وَذَلَّ كُلُّ شَيْءٍ لَكُمْ
